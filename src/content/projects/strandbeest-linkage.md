## Overview

A high-school project where I modeled and printed a Strandbeest-style walking linkage. Theo Jansen's geometry uses eleven link lengths to convert a single rotating crank into a smooth, ground-clearing leg motion — get one ratio wrong and the foot either drags or stops touching the ground.

I built it because I wanted to see if I could go from a paper diagram to a working mechanism on my own, with no kit. CAD in Fusion, parts printed and assembled with shoulder bolts and nylon spacers. The walking motion matched Jansen's reference video — gratifying confirmation that the geometry was right and the print tolerances were tight enough.
