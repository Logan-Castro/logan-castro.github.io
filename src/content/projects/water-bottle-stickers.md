A running batch of sticker sets designed in Illustrator and printed on a sticker printer. Icons, character art, brand riffs — mostly for friends' water bottles and laptops. The fun is in the design constraints: a sticker has to read at a glance and survive a dishwasher, which kills a surprising amount of detail before it kills you.
