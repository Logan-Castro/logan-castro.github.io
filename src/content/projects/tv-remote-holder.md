A two-evening fix for the recurring problem of "where are the remotes." Modeled in Fusion as two angled shelves on a flat back plate, printed in one piece, and command-stripped to the back of the common room TV so the caddy is invisible from the front but a remote-shaped hand can find it by feel.

Nothing clever about the engineering — the value was the speed. A single print run, no fasteners, no surface prep, no roommate negotiation about wall damage. The kind of build that's worth filing in the portfolio precisely because it didn't take itself seriously.
