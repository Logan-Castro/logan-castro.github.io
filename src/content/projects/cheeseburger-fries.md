Working a snack bar job, end of the night, leftover cheeseburger patties and leftover fries on the line. The play: chop the cooked patties, toss them with the fries, top with nacho cheese. The patties stayed juicy, the fries soaked up just enough, and the cheese did the rest. Fire af. Filed here as a "just for fun" card — not engineering, mostly personality.
