## Overview

A clock based on the pixelated, deliberately vague in-game clock in Minecraft — where you can roughly tell day from night but the hour and minute are intentionally ambiguous. The machining-class assignment asked for a two-operation part on the HAAS VF-series; the design choice was mine.

## CAD and CAM

Assembly modeled in Fusion. The machined face is a single brass blank with the day/night graphic milled into the front and clearance pocketed into the back for the clock movement. Two ops — front-side detail and back-side clearance — programmed in FeatureCAM with a custom fixture sketch for the second op so the workholding wouldn't fight the back-side pocketing.

## 3D printing

The face mount and clock-movement housing are 3D printed. Letting print parts handle the non-precision geometry kept the machining time scoped to the parts that actually need it — the face itself.

## Outcome

A working wall clock that's just specific enough to tell you whether to be at class or in bed, and just vague enough that it's clearly a Minecraft reference.
