The wall behind my dorm's communal kitchen trash can was taking damage from missed shots. Drew a backboard in Illustrator, laser-cut it from a piece of cardboard, and taped it to the wall. The actual hoop was already there in the form of the trash can. Quick utility build that doubled as a low-stakes game during late nights.
