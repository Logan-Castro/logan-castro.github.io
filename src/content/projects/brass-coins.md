A small batch of brass coins engraved with custom artwork. Designed the line work in Illustrator, then ran them on an xTool F1 — a fiber laser handles brass cleanly where a diode would just discolor it. Quick gift-scale project; the fun was nailing fill density and pulse settings so the engraving felt etched into the metal rather than smudged on top.
