A bedside organization piece, fully 3D printed, that hangs off the underside of the desk hutch directly beside my bed. Three things it had to do: keep my phone in a charging cradle that I could reach with one hand for alarms, hold my headphones in a dedicated slot so they weren't loose on the desk, and wear a prominent "Boston College" badge on the front so it had a little personality.

## Modular system

The interesting bit was building it as a modular family rather than a one-off. A common back plate handles the mounting and the BC badge; the front-side slots are swappable inserts sized for specific phones, earbuds, and charging cables. Once that system was in place, building roommate versions was a matter of swapping the insert plate — different phone size, different earbud type, same overall product. Three roommates, three custom versions, one underlying design.

## Why it stuck

I expected this to be a one-and-done print. The modular approach made it easy to redo when my phone changed or when the cable I was using changed — and once roommates saw theirs they started asking for tweaks. The pouch survived three relocations and was still in use through the end of the year.
