A dorm kitchen drawer that came with no organizer got a 3D-printed one. Modeled in Fusion to fit the specific drawer, printed in a few segments, and pressed flat in place. It does its job well — but it was also a reminder that 3D printing isn't always the right answer for big flat utility parts. The filament budget was embarrassing for what it produced. Next time: laser-cut acrylic or a sheet-good organizer in an evening.
