A laminated walnut "card" with laser-cut Bicycle-style Queen of Hearts artwork, made as a Valentine's gift. Drew the layered artwork in Illustrator from the original Bicycle reference, separated it into etch passes and through-cut passes, and ran it on the laser. Glued up as a thin laminate so the artwork could survive being held without falling apart.
