## Overview

A grouped case study of small CNC router builds I've used to learn the machine. Each one is a one-evening or one-weekend project, and the value is the sum: a portfolio of toolpaths, materials, finishes, and finishing-touch decisions that I can reach back to when I need a starting point for a bigger build.

## Selected pieces

- **Quantum waveform oak carve.** Plotted a wavefunction and toolpath-carved it into red oak. The math is the point — the carve is a way to feel a shape that's otherwise abstract.
- **Catch-all tray.** Walnut, finger-jointed corners, V-carved divider. Lives by my front door.
- **Shop signage.** A handful of pieces for friends' workshops. Most were Illustrator → Carbide Create → router with a finishing pass to soften the edges.
- **Resin-coated coasters.** CNC-pocketed cherry, tinted resin pours, sanded flat. Silicone mold for batch pours.
- **Man cave sign.** Routed letters in maple, lined with reflective vinyl, embedded LED strip in the back cavity. Frosted acrylic diffuser hid the hotspots.

## Why a collection

Most of these aren't interesting individually — but the iteration across them taught me feeds and speeds, dust collection limits, finishing tradeoffs, and which kinds of mistakes recover gracefully and which don't. A grouped page is more honest than pretending each was its own deep project.
