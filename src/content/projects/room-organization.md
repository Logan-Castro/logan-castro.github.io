An ongoing series of small organization builds for my desk and workspace. The latest piece is a desk shelf — I'd been looking at the popular Workspace Hero kit, decided not to pay for it, and redesigned my own with a couple of changes I wanted. A pair of Ikea Skadis boards just went up too, which means a steady flow of printed mounts, hooks, and bins to come.

The fun of an ongoing organization project is that the requirements actually change month-to-month as new tools land or as my workflow shifts. Treating it as one continuous project instead of a series of disconnected prints means each new mount inherits a consistent visual language.
