A pool basketball hoop for my parents' backyard, drawing heavily on Halo Ball. The first iteration spanned the full width of the yard fence-to-fence, anchored with screw eyes — playable but a little permanent for what it is.

The next iteration is happening this week for a hangout my little brother wants to host. The constraint: I can't anchor it to anything fixed. The plan is to borrow from suspension bridges — hang the hoop between two umbrellas using a tension network so the umbrellas brace each other without toppling. Sketching it out now, woodshop and a few 3D prints to come.
