A long-arc concept: start from a stock used bike and rebuild it one component at a time until every part on the frame was made by me. The point isn't the finished bike, it's the forcing function — each swap is a new skill to learn or a new fabrication problem to solve, and the bike has to stay ride-ready throughout so the failure modes stay honest.

Likely starting with hardware (axles, dropouts) before working up to drivetrain and eventually brazed frame tubes. CAD model of the donor frame is where I'll begin.
