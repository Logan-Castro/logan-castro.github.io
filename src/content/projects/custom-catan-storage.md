A planned organizer for the pieces of a Catan set — every hex, number tile, resource card, road, settlement, and city in a single wooden case that opens flat to a usable surface. Stretch goal: integrate a folding board so the case becomes the play surface and the whole game packs into a single object.

Still in the sketching stage. The interesting design constraints are around hex tile retention (Catan's hexes shift between games and lose their place fast) and around making the folding mechanism survive being shoved into a backpack.
