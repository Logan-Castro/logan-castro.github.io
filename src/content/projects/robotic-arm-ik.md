Sitting in class with idle time, I derived the inverse kinematics for a 3-axis robotic arm — the Cartesian-to-polar conversion and the joint-angle solving you'd need to drive an end effector to a point in space. The math came first; the hardware doesn't exist yet.

Planning to validate the equations in Python before building anything physical. CAD, 3D printing, and basic circuitry come later — once I can prove the IK in simulation, the build becomes the easy part.
