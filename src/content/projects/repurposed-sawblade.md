A retired table saw blade with a chipped tooth got a second life as a display piece. Drew the cut path in Illustrator, waterjet-cut a logo into the body of the blade, and let the original teeth stay as-is — the contrast between the precise waterjet cut and the worn factory teeth is what makes the piece work.
