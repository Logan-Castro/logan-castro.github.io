Still in concept. I want to build a small instrument package to monitor coastal water — temperature, salinity, possibly turbidity — somewhere on the California coast, probably Tomales Bay or somewhere similar.

The form factor is genuinely open. It might float, might sink to a known depth, might drift, might be tethered. Each option flips the entire problem (power budget, antenna, drag, recovery), and the right answer depends on what data I actually want first. Likely ESP32 at the core, low-power electronics throughout, 3D-printed housing.

The early phase is going to be more about asking the right questions than building anything.
